The Breinrock Payment Network: Local Payments Within Global Hubs

Send and receive local currency payments within the UAE, UK, EU, US, and Canada.

Breinrock gives you the speed and reliability of domestic banking while operating on a global scale.

Contact Us

Key Features & Benefits

Act Local in Global Markets

Pay and receive in AED, GBP, EUR, USD, and CAD as if you were a local business in each region.

Faster Settlement

Avoid international wire delays. Payments are processed through local rails for quicker access to funds.

Cost-Effective

Reduce the expense of traditional cross-border transfers by using local payment infrastructure.

Trusted Infrastructure

Built on regulated partnerships and Breinrock’s secure platform, ensuring reliability and compliance in every transaction.

Personal and Corporate Ready

Whether settling invoices, managing suppliers, or receiving client payments, the network adapts to both business and personal needs.

How It Works

1. Open Your Breinrock Account

Get access to dedicated accounts in your supported jurisdictions.

2. Send or Receive Locally

Pay in local currencies within the UAE, UK, EU, US, or Canada using domestic payment rails.

3. Manage from One Platform

Monitor balances, exchange if needed, and make further transfers or card payments directly from your Breinrock dashboard.

Expert Guidance and Support

Every client has a dedicated relationship manager to guide them through onboarding and payment structuring. Whether it is managing supplier networks in Europe or receiving client funds in North America, you always have expert support.
