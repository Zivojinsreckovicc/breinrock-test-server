Our Products

Breinrock offers innovative fintech products, from corporate and personal accounts to prepaid cards, foreign exchange, BaaS, and digital assets, tailored to meet your financial needs.

Explore More

Corporate and Personal Accounts

Explore a comprehensive payment experience that caters to both corporate and personal accounts. Our robust suite of products encompasses local and cross-border payments, foreign exchange services, corporate prepaid cards, and alternative payment methods.

Learn More

Rock "N"Pay Prepaid Cards

Receive your prepaid card for domestic and international use. Use your card at any ATM that displays the Mastercard® acceptance marks. Keep track of your spending online.

Learn More

Foreign Exchange

Exchange online with ease. Whether you're managing personal finances, running a business, or investing internationally, Breinrock has you covered. From USD, GBP, CAD, AED, EUR, our platform allows you to carry out all your FX transactions in minutes.

Learn More

Banking as a Service (BaaS)

Transform your business with Breinrock's white-label Banking as a Service platform. Embed financial services directly into your products, from payment processing to account management, all under your brand.

Learn More

Digital Assets

Access the crypto market through Breinrock's secure Digital Assets service. Convert EUR or USD into leading cryptocurrencies including USDC, USDT, ETH, BTC, LTC, BRNC, and BNB. Manage fiat and crypto from one integrated platform.
