Provide your clients with accounts, payments, the ability to exchange currencies, as well as other financial services without having to be a fully-licensed bank.

Banking-as-a-Service (BaaS) is a model that allows companies to offer a full suite of banking services without needing to become fully licensed banks. With Breinrock, we provide the entire banking platform, enabling businesses to deliver financial services directly to their customers.

BaaS works by partnering with a licensed bank or provider like Breinrock, who offers access to banking infrastructure via APIs, allowing your business to offer financial services.
